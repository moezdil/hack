Live captions for conference talks. Scan the code on the speaker's slide, read along on your phone. No app, no account, nothing stored.

Built for every listenerDeaf and hard of hearing, non-native speakers, the back row. Big text and high contrast, one tap away.
Nothing to installA link on any phone. Captions stream over the open web; the room stays on the speaker's side.
Honest about delayEach phone shows how far behind it is. The same number drives the speaker's dashboard.
Speakers need Chrome: speech recognition runs in the browser and audio goes to Google's speech service. Open source on GitHub.
