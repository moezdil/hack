Live captions for conference talks. Scan the code on the speaker's slide and read along on your own phone. No app, no account, nothing stored.

Scan. Read. Belong.

Built for every listener

Deaf and hard of hearing, non-native speakers, the back row. Big text and high contrast, one tap away.

Nothing to install

One link on any phone. Captions stream over the open web; the mic stays on the speaker's side.

Honest about delay

Each phone shows how far behind it is. The same number drives the speaker's dashboard.

#hallway Reading along next to someone? Say hi. The best conversations start with "did you catch that?"
#speakers Put the QR on your first slide and your last one. Late joiners get the last 50 lines.
#organizers One room per track, one QR per screen. Costs about nothing per talk.
Speakers need Chrome: speech recognition runs in the browser and audio goes to Google's speech service. Source on GitHub. Made at the Cloud Native Hackathon, Salt Lake City.
